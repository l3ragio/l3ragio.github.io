MCK Properties

MCK Properties

Which properties can a declared observer represent, evaluate, and attribute to an agent or artifact?

MCK is the property layer of the research program. It develops an observer-relative vocabulary for meaning, residual opacity, function, and agent property attribution before those properties are evaluated over time.

Research programNext: LTL Evaluation

Research profile

Object of study

Artifacts and agents described relative to an observer, an evidence regime, and a declared set of structural and semantic predicates.

Core vocabulary

Meaning, residual opacity, function, region membership, restricted transitions, structural carriers, and semantic calibration.

Agent properties

Identity, integrity, and freedom are treated as candidate formal targets only when their carriers and evidence assumptions are explicit.

Methods

Observer-relative classification, finite region systems, transition mechanisms, two-layer agent state, and finite decision procedures.

Why this layer comes first

A temporal monitor can only evaluate predicates that have already been made precise enough to observe. MCK therefore precedes the temporal layer: it asks what the candidate property is, what carries it, which observer can recognize it, and what evidence supports the attribution.

The framework does not treat a property name as its own measurement. An agent may have a structurally realizable carrier while the semantic content assigned to that carrier remains uncalibrated. Keeping those layers separate prevents a formal-looking label from silently becoming an empirical result.

Public research sequence

  1. Classification. The foundation paper defines an observer-relative region system over meaning, residual opacity, and functionality.
  2. Transitions. The transition paper asks which changes between regions can be realized by restricted mechanisms and where those mechanisms stop.
  3. Self-attribution. The agent paper separates structural realizability from semantic calibration when an agent attributes identity, integrity, or freedom to itself.

Relationship to the wider program

LTL Evaluation takes declared properties and asks what finite or continuing trace evidence can justify about them. ChronoSpec then tests whether explicit temporal contracts can also shape model behavior, while LTL Triangles studies their use in post-failure governance.

Current status and claim boundary

MCK Properties is a public preprint cluster. The page does not claim peer-review acceptance, universal definitions of agent properties, or that the proposed signatures are already validated as mechanistic measures.

Preprint, 2026 / MCK Properties

When Can an Agent Attribute Properties to Itself? A Cognitive Algebra for Identity, Integrity, and Freedom

Davide Bragetti; Silvio Micali; Giuseppe Francesco Italiano

Zenodo preprint, version 3

Develops the MCK self-attribution layer with a two-layer state separating structural carriers from semantic content, then studies continuity, viability, and owned branching as property signatures.

Paper page
ABS

An agent's cognitive architecture may possess every regional carrier required for a capability and still emit a wrong verdict, because the content those carriers hold is corrupted. Over the MCK observer-relative classification (Bragetti, Asaro and Italiano, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21772772), version 2 formalises the boundary between what an architecture can structurally realise and what it can semantically get right.

The central object is a two-layer state kappa_a(t) = (Sigma_a(t), Delta_a(t)): regional carriers versus denotational and evidence content. A capability decomposes into structural requirements (Req-Sigma) and content-correctness requirements (Req-Delta), realisability being their conjunction; this yields a three-way status (structurally untypable / typable-but-incorrect / realised) and proves that structural extension is monotone in carrier presence but not in semantic calibration.

Six constructions follow: an attribute-certificate calculus for the step-relation licence slot; a self-symbol discharging the epistemogenesis conjecture and anchoring de se and reflexive certification; an other-model triad (modelled agent, modelled observation regime, predicted monitor) with the actual observation-relative monitor imported from Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21700205; an operator-composition typing separating structural from semantic composability; a bounded transition system making Cert / MetaCert / calibrated-world reachability decidable at finite scale; and a bounded integrity-consistency decision procedure in O(n^2 c) time.

Three worked self-properties (continuity, viability, owned branching - glossed identity, integrity, freedom) receive native two-layer signatures with incomparable structural and calibration blast radii. An attention layer completes the architecture: attention as an explicit third configuration coordinate, focus shifts as a declared transition family, a same-state / different-focus theorem, an attention-gated feedback channel over temporal implication, and the activation-signature analysis: of the 32 signatures combining structural realisability, semantic adequacy, attention, reflexive availability, and behavioural expression, exactly 18 are type-consistent, and no link of the naive activation chain holds. Applied to four cognitive worlds, the two-layer state proves same-Sigma / different-Delta non-injectivity.

Every finite model, witness and countermodel is exhaustively validated by the checker suite distributed with the article (v1 suite retained without regression; extended regional checker, mechanised dependency-graph extractor, and figure-consistency checker over a single-source visual specification). Version 2 supersedes v1 as an extension; v1 remains available at this concept DOI.

Preprint, 2026 / MCK Properties

MCK Region Transitions: Restricted Realization Mechanisms and Their Limits

Davide Bragetti; Edoardo Palumbo; Carmelo Asaro; Giuseppe Francesco Italiano

Zenodo preprint

Studies restricted mechanisms for moving artifacts across MCK regions, including assimilation, operator admission, denotation, and reconstruction certificates.

Paper page
ABS

The MCK framework partitions artifacts into seven regions by three predicates — meaning, opacity, function — and its region transitions form a group acting on the region set. The result is algebraic: it exhibits the transition between any two regions and supplies no procedure that moves an artifact along one. We study four restricted realization families and fix, for each, what it covers and where it stops.

Assimilation reclassifies pure opacity and nothing else; we prove that the residue — the opacity co-present with meaning or with function — is a floor no comprehension strength lowers, so an agent that possesses symbols cannot assimilate its way out of possessing them. Operator admission seats a candidate in the fully intersecting region without extending structural reach, so any extension of reach under a fixed realization witnesses a changed basis rather than a new name. Denotation admits as sources exactly the symbol domain and is not recoverable from the predicates: perfect recovery of a region label leaves denotation undetermined. The reconstruction certificate is strictly weaker than the discovery predicate, the gap being a key-membership condition we show unclosable in general.

Each mechanism is studied for a declared observer; no universal artifact-level realizer follows. The realization is ideal and exact; whether a trained representation supports an approximate counterpart is not addressed here, and no infinite-reachability claim rests on finite evidence. Five temporal conjectures over locally declared runs mark open trace-level questions; no monitor is constructed.

---

RELATIONSHIP TO "MEANING, OPACITY, AND FUNCTION" (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21772772).

FOUNDATIONAL IMPORT. The seven-region partition, the three predicates, the structured ambiguity domain, the suspension convention on the purely functional cell, the semantic horizon, and the denotation map are taken from that paper and used exactly as defined there. They are not re-derived, and no embedding or reduction between the two settings is claimed.

EXTENSION. That framework establishes, for any ordered pair of regions, that a unique transition between them exists, and supplies no procedure that carries a given artifact along one. This paper studies four restricted realization mechanisms rather than the general case, and fixes for each what it covers and where it stops. Nothing here closes that gap.

NEW RESULTS. The invariance of the symbol domain under assimilation and the residual-opacity floor; the admission of an operator without extension of structural reach; the non-recoverability of denotation from the predicates; the key-membership gap between the reconstruction certificate and the discovery predicate; and the denotation graph with its four modes and fourteen canonical symbol classes, which discharges a deferral the foundations paper makes by name.

CONJECTURES. Five statements written with temporal operators over locally declared runs. All are conjectural or explicitly open, none is used in a proof, and no monitor is constructed.

Preprint, 2026 / MCK Properties

Meaning, Opacity, and Function: An Observer-Relative Classification of Artifacts

Davide Bragetti; Carmelo Asaro; Giuseppe Francesco Italiano

Zenodo preprint

Defines the observer-relative MCK region system over meaning, residual opacity, and functionality, fixing the vocabulary used by later MCK work.

Paper page
ABS

Whether an artifact is understood, merely apprehended, or functional depends on the observer confronting it rather than on the artifact alone. We take three binary predicates over a domain of artifacts – structured meaning M, residual opacity C, and functionality K, each read relative to an agent – and study the finite datum they induce: which of the seven regions they carve are inhabited for that agent.

Two modelling choices are stipulated before anything is derived: the semantic predicates need not exclude one another, and denotation is confined to their overlap Θ = M ∩ C. The admissible data are then exactly the covers of the predicate set, so their number – 109 labelled, 34 up to relabelling – is the classical cover count of a three-element set and not a new enumeration.

Three results follow. The types with empty symbolic carrier are exactly those omitting the two overlap cells, seventeen in number; since a query's denoting token must lie in that carrier – a requirement that is definitional – an agent of such a type poses no query whatever architecture it carries. The six-coordinate structural profile that a type fixes is realizable if and only if it satisfies four explicit Boolean conditions, whence 39 of the 64 profiles occur. And architectures inducing the same compositional closure may still differ in the generation depth they assign its elements and in the pairs on which their composition is defined, two being the least size of the functional set at which this can happen. The predicates are supplied model data throughout: identifying the intended assignment from external evidence lies outside the present formalism.
