Research

Preprint, 2026 / MCK Properties

When Can an Agent Attribute Properties to Itself? A Cognitive Algebra for Identity, Integrity, and Freedom

Davide Bragetti; Silvio Micali; Giuseppe Francesco Italiano

Zenodo preprint, version 3

Develops the MCK self-attribution layer with a two-layer state separating structural carriers from semantic content, then studies continuity, viability, and owned branching as property signatures.

Paper page
ABS

An agent's cognitive architecture may possess every regional carrier required for a capability and still emit a wrong verdict, because the content those carriers hold is corrupted. Over the MCK observer-relative classification (Bragetti, Asaro and Italiano, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21772772), version 2 formalises the boundary between what an architecture can structurally realise and what it can semantically get right.

The central object is a two-layer state kappa_a(t) = (Sigma_a(t), Delta_a(t)): regional carriers versus denotational and evidence content. A capability decomposes into structural requirements (Req-Sigma) and content-correctness requirements (Req-Delta), realisability being their conjunction; this yields a three-way status (structurally untypable / typable-but-incorrect / realised) and proves that structural extension is monotone in carrier presence but not in semantic calibration.

Six constructions follow: an attribute-certificate calculus for the step-relation licence slot; a self-symbol discharging the epistemogenesis conjecture and anchoring de se and reflexive certification; an other-model triad (modelled agent, modelled observation regime, predicted monitor) with the actual observation-relative monitor imported from Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.21700205; an operator-composition typing separating structural from semantic composability; a bounded transition system making Cert / MetaCert / calibrated-world reachability decidable at finite scale; and a bounded integrity-consistency decision procedure in O(n^2 c) time.

Three worked self-properties (continuity, viability, owned branching - glossed identity, integrity, freedom) receive native two-layer signatures with incomparable structural and calibration blast radii. An attention layer completes the architecture: attention as an explicit third configuration coordinate, focus shifts as a declared transition family, a same-state / different-focus theorem, an attention-gated feedback channel over temporal implication, and the activation-signature analysis: of the 32 signatures combining structural realisability, semantic adequacy, attention, reflexive availability, and behavioural expression, exactly 18 are type-consistent, and no link of the naive activation chain holds. Applied to four cognitive worlds, the two-layer state proves same-Sigma / different-Delta non-injectivity.

Every finite model, witness and countermodel is exhaustively validated by the checker suite distributed with the article (v1 suite retained without regression; extended regional checker, mechanised dependency-graph extractor, and figure-consistency checker over a single-source visual specification). Version 2 supersedes v1 as an extension; v1 remains available at this concept DOI.

Preprint, 2026 / MCK Properties

MCK Region Transitions: Restricted Realization Mechanisms and Their Limits

Davide Bragetti; Edoardo Palumbo; Carmelo Asaro; Giuseppe Francesco Italiano

Zenodo preprint

Studies restricted mechanisms for moving artifacts across MCK regions, including assimilation, operator admission, denotation, and reconstruction certificates.

Paper page
ABS

The MCK framework partitions artifacts into seven regions by three predicates — meaning, opacity, function — and its region transitions form a group acting on the region set. The result is algebraic: it exhibits the transition between any two regions and supplies no procedure that moves an artifact along one. We study four restricted realization families and fix, for each, what it covers and where it stops.

Assimilation reclassifies pure opacity and nothing else; we prove that the residue — the opacity co-present with meaning or with function — is a floor no comprehension strength lowers, so an agent that possesses symbols cannot assimilate its way out of possessing them. Operator admission seats a candidate in the fully intersecting region without extending structural reach, so any extension of reach under a fixed realization witnesses a changed basis rather than a new name. Denotation admits as sources exactly the symbol domain and is not recoverable from the predicates: perfect recovery of a region label leaves denotation undetermined. The reconstruction certificate is strictly weaker than the discovery predicate, the gap being a key-membership condition we show unclosable in general.

Each mechanism is studied for a declared observer; no universal artifact-level realizer follows. The realization is ideal and exact; whether a trained representation supports an approximate counterpart is not addressed here, and no infinite-reachability claim rests on finite evidence. Five temporal conjectures over locally declared runs mark open trace-level questions; no monitor is constructed.

Preprint, 2026 / MCK Properties

Meaning, Opacity, and Function: An Observer-Relative Classification of Artifacts

Davide Bragetti; Carmelo Asaro; Giuseppe Francesco Italiano

Zenodo preprint

Defines the observer-relative MCK region system over meaning, residual opacity, and functionality, fixing the vocabulary used by later MCK work.

Paper page
ABS

Whether an artifact is understood, merely apprehended, or functional depends on the observer confronting it rather than on the artifact alone. We take three binary predicates over a domain of artifacts – structured meaning M, residual opacity C, and functionality K, each read relative to an agent – and study the finite datum they induce: which of the seven regions they carve are inhabited for that agent.

Two modelling choices are stipulated before anything is derived: the semantic predicates need not exclude one another, and denotation is confined to their overlap Θ = M ∩ C. The admissible data are then exactly the covers of the predicate set, so their number – 109 labelled, 34 up to relabelling – is the classical cover count of a three-element set and not a new enumeration.

Three results follow. The types with empty symbolic carrier are exactly those omitting the two overlap cells, seventeen in number; since a query's denoting token must lie in that carrier – a requirement that is definitional – an agent of such a type poses no query whatever architecture it carries. The six-coordinate structural profile that a type fixes is realizable if and only if it satisfies four explicit Boolean conditions, whence 39 of the 64 profiles occur. And architectures inducing the same compositional closure may still differ in the generation depth they assign its elements and in the pairs on which their composition is defined, two being the least size of the functional set at which this can happen. The predicates are supplied model data throughout: identifying the intended assignment from external evidence lies outside the present formalism.

Working paper, 2026 / Temporal Evaluation

The Intersection Algebra of Safety, Cosafety, Liveness, and Coliveness over Linear Temporal Logic

Davide Bragetti

Zenodo working paper

Classifies omega-regular properties through temporal evidence classes and connects the classification to verdict alphabets for runtime monitoring.

Paper page
ABS

Runtime verification of linear-temporal-logic (LTL) properties needs a precise classification of which omega-regular properties can be monitored, in what verdict alphabet, and with what residual evidence on the unverified portion. Alpern and Schneider partitioned properties into safety and liveness; Kupferman and Vardi added cosafety; coliveness completes the symmetric structure. We integrate these four families into a single intersection algebra over omega-regular properties, develop its operational counterpart as a verdict-alphabet ladder spanning binary, ternary, four-valued, and quantitative monitor systems, and connect the two layers through a universal-attribution theorem.

On the classification side, the four families generate a sixteen-cell exclusive partition of the omega-regular properties under combined topological and prefix-quantifier membership. The partition is equivalent, modulo two degenerate singletons, to the nine-cell verdict-axis partition of Peled and Havelund. We give independent topological and prefix-quantifier derivations of the empty cells, a De Morgan duality structure on the partition induced by the LTL temporal dualities, and a literature-anchored worked-example catalog with provenance per cell.

On the operational side, a verdict-alphabet ladder spans verdict cardinalities one, two, three, four, and infinity (with the trivial baseline at one, the qualitative rungs at two, three, and four, and the quantitative limit at infinity). Definitive verdicts on every trace characterise the clopen cell, and on every violation or every satisfaction the safety or cosafety properties, which the three-valued rung covers in a single monitor; there definitive verdicts saturate: the four-valued and quantitative rungs add revocable and quantitative information but resolve no further cell, and monitorability in the sense of Pnueli and Zaks is not a function of the cell. A universal-attribution theorem shows that every non-trivial verdict monitor canonically yields, for each verdict, a persistence pullback property on its input, the pre-image of a Liveness-and-Coliveness property of its verdict trace; this pullback is itself Liveness-and-Coliveness exactly when, after every input prefix, the verdict trace can still both settle on that verdict and fail to settle on it. The framework supplies a precise vocabulary for the calibration analysis of monitor-based epistemic guarantees on omega-regular properties.

Preprint, 2026 / Temporal Evaluation

Touch and Crossing: Dual Primitive Modalities of Threshold Monitors

Davide Bragetti

Zenodo preprint

Argues that the touch and crossing modalities of threshold monitors are dual primitives with incomparable minimal resource requirements, and orders the tuning powers that realise the verdict-alphabet rungs.

Paper page
ABS

Threshold monitors of real-valued signals fire on one of two operational semantics: the crossing modality, with events at upward transits of a threshold, or the touch modality, with events at local extrema. The runtime-verification and signal-processing literatures treat these as competing encoder families, usually presenting one as primitive and the other as a degenerate case. We argue that this framing is wrong: touch and crossing are dual primitives with incomparable minimal resource requirements - crossing is causal and carries one bit of memory, whereas touch is memoryless but needs a three-sample window and one step of lookahead - so neither monitor can simulate the other without genuinely new structure, and bridging them costs exactly one added bit of structure in each direction: persistent state one way, window or lookahead the other.

We develop three consequences. First, the two modalities sit on a ladder indexed by the number of distinct verdicts a monitor may emit: a verdict-free baseline, a degenerate binary rung, a ternary rung that hosts the touch/crossing duality, and a four-valued rung that organises both along orthogonal touch/crossing and weak/strong axes. The two-, three-, and four-valued rungs themselves are the known runtime-verification verdict progression (the two-/three-/four-valued LTL semantics); our contribution at this layer is not the ladder but the strict tuning hierarchy L1 < L2 < L3 of tuning powers that realise the rungs. Second, the touch-crossing join is a literal parameter coincidence at the four-valued rung: a single augmented monitor realises both modalities as exact parameter choices, and the three classical engineering encoders - edge detector, Schmitt trigger, and extremum detector - appear as named special cases of its ternary subfamily. Third, the duality is operational: a pointwise count inequality (upward crossings never outnumber touches, up to a trace-boundary tick) separates the two on every signal; a linear-time algorithm synthesises tuning parameters realising any feasible target finite event pattern, with an asymmetric feasibility criterion; and a safety / cosafety / liveness / coliveness classification places both modalities inside the existing temporal-property landscape without new logical machinery.

Preprint, 2026 / Temporal Evaluation

Observation-Relative Monitorability of Bounded Temporal Implication

Davide Bragetti; Alessandro Bragetti; Giuseppe F. Italiano

Zenodo preprint

Shows that monitorability of bounded temporal implication is joint in the formula and observation model, not a property of the formula alone.

Paper page
ABS

A material conditional "P implies Q" has a timeless truth value, but knowing whether it holds does not: every physical or computational check of the conditional is an act of observation, carried out at finite resolution, with each event separated from its observation by delay, drift, and jitter. This produces a paradox of observed implication -- an internally sound monitor can contradict the timeless truth value in both directions (a spurious falsifier, or a phantom trigger), not because the instrument is broken but because observation has a class.

We make this precise for the temporal implication G(p -> F[<=k] q) and establish a single headline result: the monitorability of the bounded implication is joint in the pair (formula, observation model) -- the observation model being the sampling regime through which the signals are read (when the monitor looks, and the delay, drift, jitter, and finite resolution between an event and its observation) -- and is not a property of the formula alone. A change of observation model alone can move the property across the safety / cosafety / liveness / coliveness classification. The observation space partitions into a machine-checked taxonomy of eight classes; a monotone ladder of declared observation theories grades which classes become True-attributable; and internally sound observers that disagree hold different rungs rather than contradicting each other.

Preprint; a version has been prepared for submission. Engineering companion: "Controlling the Observation Class of a Runtime Monitor" (co-deposited).

Controlling the Observation Class of a Runtime Monitor: Selection, Self-Tuning, and Physical Grounding

Davide Bragetti; Carlo Bragetti; Giuseppe F. Italiano

Zenodo preprint

Engineering companion showing how a runtime monitor's observation class can be selected and locally tuned under physical and causal-coherence constraints.

Paper page
ABS

Engineering companion establishing that a runtime monitor's observation class is a controlled quantity. Where prior runtime verification fixes the observation class, this paper steers it, for a bounded-eventuality temporal implication. Three layers, over a machine-checked generative signature space (the 390625 -> 50000 -> 8 eight-tick enumeration):

(1) Optimal monitor-type selection over the exhaustively enumerated catalogue, minimising diagnostic ambiguity and opener sensitivity, with the finest timing-error resolution among the minimisers and an analytic stability radius.

(2) A closed self-tuning loop recovering timing phase by classical symbol-synchronisation machinery (early-late gate, Gardner, Mueller-Mueller detectors). The proven result is almost-sure LOCAL convergence to the true phase within a basin of attraction; global convergence of the band-counting detector is CONJECTURED and witnessed only on a finite simulation prefix.

(3) A causal-coherence penalty forbidding convergence to an epistemically invalid operating point -- the physical boundary that keeps tuning from manufacturing truth.

Refinement-Based Confirmation of Liveness Properties from Monitor Verdict Traces: A Hensel-Newton Approach

Davide Bragetti

Zenodo preprint

Studies what extra refinement structure can supply when a finite monitor prefix cannot by itself confirm a liveness property over a continuing execution.

Paper page
ABS

A runtime monitor that observes a finite prefix of an execution can often refute a property but never confirm it, or the reverse, and for some properties it can do neither. We study what an external structure attached to such a monitor has to supply in order to recover the missing verdict, and we introduce a refinement system for that purpose: a chain of candidate configurations, a computable update rule, and a computable error metric that measures how far the current configuration is from certifying the target. When the error metric decreases by a fixed positive quantum at every step at which it is positive, the target is certified within a bound determined by the initial error and that quantum.

The main point of the analysis is a separation the argument makes visible. Finite descent yields a statement about a prefix. Turning it into a statement about the infinite execution requires two further ingredients, stated and used separately: that the observed execution belongs to a declared class of admitted behaviours, and, for the persistent form, that the certifying condition once reached is never lost. Neither follows from the descent.

Modular inversion by Hensel lifting is the worked instance: the residual squares at each step, so a certified lower bound on the initial precision yields a guaranteed deadline for confirmation, and the confirming condition is absorbing, which supplies the temporal lift in full. Five primality methods are placed against the same scheme as a scope study. Enforcement by adapting monitor parameters rather than editing the observed trace is defined and left open.

Autoimmune Rationalization: A Protective-Systems Analogy and a Formal Mechanism for Self-Defeating Epistemic Protection

Davide Bragetti

Zenodo preprint

Shows that "I believe only what I can prove", read as an acceptance policy over finite confirmation, can defeat the system it protects without a single false verdict, and characterises when such protection is self-defeating.

Paper page
ABS

"I believe only what I can prove" sounds like the safest rule a reasoning agent can adopt. We show that, as an acceptance policy, it can defeat the system it protects without a single false verdict. A protective architecture turns finite evidence into verdicts, verdicts into an operational policy, and the policy into the opening or closing of the system's own capabilities, on which its objective depends. Evidence is modelled as refinement relative to a declared class of admitted behaviours: a record can confirm a commitment, refute it, or leave it undecided, and coliveness commitments, such as recurrences and responses, can never be confirmed by any finite record although they may be true. Read with "prove" as finite confirmation, the cardinal policy is default-reject: it treats "undecided" as "closed", so it never relies on such commitments and keeps the capabilities they gate closed at every time. Confirming them within a narrower context is no lasting remedy: when the context changes, such confirmations can dissolve, and coliveness can return. In a finite game arena we prove that when those capabilities are counterfactually necessary for the objective, the objective becomes unenforceable. We call such protection self-defeating when an intended strategy that relies on the protected commitments only where they hold would have won, and separate it from protective trade-offs and from the correct rejection of false commitments. When all the commitments are coliveness and sustaining, true on every course of events that achieves the objective, the trade-off case disappears: the cardinal policy is then self-defeating exactly when the capabilities it closes are necessary support, and for every strategy that would have won. Self-defeating protection is the formal object; autoimmune rationalization is our name for its reading in an epistemic agent (a rational criterion defeating its own ends), a bounded analogy with selected features of autoimmunity, not an immunological or psychological claim. In its reflexive case, where the agent's reason is read as sustained by the condition its objective secures, the cardinal policy withdraws reliance from that very condition. We also separate the correctness of a criterion from its authority to decide and from the priority of the goods it protects, show that loss through a dependency between protected properties (a protective inversion) coincides in the game model with necessary support of the protected property, and read narratives of the Christian tradition as interpretive cases. Readings in terms of reason, belief, and drama-triangle roles are interpretive.

What is new in version RC20. The paper is re-centred on the cardinal policy "I believe only what I can prove". A new Section 7.5 defines sustaining commitments (Definition 7.10) and proves that for commitments that are coliveness and sustaining every winning strategy is reliance-safe, so that the architecture is self-defeating exactly when the gated moves are necessary support, and then for every strategy that would have won (Proposition 7.11); Corollary 7.12 applies this to the finite-confirmation policy. An instance on the reference arena, drawn in a new figure, shows the victim lock as a consequence of the cardinal policy. An interpretive reflexive case reads the policy, under Assumption 5.4 with the objective's own condition as ground, as an operational defeater of the condition on which its holder rests. The paper now positions the result against the classical self-refutation of comprehensive rationalism (Bartley), against Clifford's evidentialism, against proof-based agents in AI safety (the procrastination paradox), and against Parfit's notion of self-defeating theories: the rule here is consistent and every verdict is correct, and what is lost is the enforceability of the objective. All statements and numbers of RC19 are unchanged. The philosophical and anthropological contribution of Paolo Benanti (LUISS Guido Carli) is acknowledged in the manuscript.

What is new in version RC21. The title of RC18-RC19 is restored. A new Proposition 7.13 (Context revision) shows that confirmation is relative to the agent's working context: a confirmation relative to all behaviours survives every change of context, while a recurrence that a narrower context lets the agent confirm (for instance one in which what is restored stays restored) is coliveness relative to all behaviours; when the context widens, the confirmation dissolves and default-reject closes the gate again. Recurrences and responses therefore have no finite certainty that is not borrowed from a context, a further reason to keep indefinitely extended commitments in view. All statements, numbers and proofs of RC20 are unchanged.

Intentional Semantic Geometry: Statements as Grammar-Mediated References into Determinable Spectra

Davide Bragetti; Paolo Spagnoletti; Giuseppe Francesco Italiano

Zenodo preprint

Proposes Intentional Semantic Geometry: statements as grammar-mediated pointers into semantic spectra, with a typed calculus for the gap a partition leaves and for its reintegration.

Paper page
ABS

Non-trivial scientific meaning is not, in general, a proposition in a fixed logic-space. A statement is a pointer into a semantic geometry - a spectrum - and its interpretation is grammar-mediated: a grammar decides how the statement names regions or positions in the spectrum. Both the pointing (where does the statement land?) and the substrate (what is the spectrum, structurally?) are objects of research. We propose Intentional Semantic Geometry (ISG) as the discipline that makes this structure explicit: statements, grammars, spectra, references, and two irreducible modes of indeterminacy (pointing indeterminacy under a fixed substrate, substrate indeterminacy across candidate grammars). Under ISG, the operators DIA (which names the interpretive gap left by a partition) and REI (which reintegrates that gap) recover a natural position as the derived discipline for handling the double indeterminacy. We equip them with a typed calculus that separates the type of an operation from the direction in which it is applied and from its normative valence, and that identifies which reintegration routes transform a state and which only certify one. The same calculus types the two deployment effects of a separating criterion - excisive cutting and non-excisive classification, which refines the description of an intact spectrum - and states the typed interface through which classified, still-unnamed regions become inputs to symbolic reification in the companion MCK framework. The framework unifies substrates already studied independently in the programme (topological property classes over infinite traces; epistemic region cubes; capability lattices for threshold monitors; two-agent channel-based semantic bodies), and provides the language for a reflexive application: a paper's positioning statements about its own contribution are ISG operations on the contribution's admissible-readings spectrum, subject to the same double indeterminacy as any other statement about a geometrically-supported object.

One Number Per Cell: A Separating-Family Encoding of Superimposed Discrete Curves

Davide Bragetti

Zenodo preprint

Encodes several superimposed discrete curves with a single static number per cell, read as a member of a separating family, and proves the local-indistinguishability boundary for state-blind tracers.

Paper page
ABS

On a discrete grid several curves may cross and share cells, yet a tracer following one curve must recover, at every shared cell, where ITS OWN curve continues. We ask for a single static number per cell --- no stored lists --- from which each curve reads its next step with no collision. The answer is to read the cell number as a member of a SEPARATING FAMILY: assign curve i the prime p_i and store the product of p_i raised to curve i's local Freeman direction; each curve recovers its direction as the exponent of its own prime, and unique factorisation guarantees that distinct curves never interfere.

We analyse the cost of this prime code and give more compact separating families --- the residue number system (Chinese Remainder Theorem), positional mixed-radix, and bit-packing --- that share the same properties without factorisation; the prime form is space-dominated. The result is sharp: a local, state-blind tracer cannot resolve a tangency (two curves entering a cell alike but leaving differently) by any cell number, so the irreducible resource is a per-curve index, of order log m bits, carried in the tracer, not in the cell (the local-indistinguishability theorem). The construction has a mutable dual: the rotor-router places the same disambiguating resource inside the cell and advances it on each visit. Separating family and rotor-router are the two escapes of the impossibility boundary. Simple curves --- open paths, closed loops, and rho-shapes --- are admissible; only genuine self-intersection is excluded, with a branch-separation (residue-jet) extension outlined for it.

An interactive demonstration runs at https://l3ragio.github.io/one-number-per-cell/ (source: https://github.com/l3ragio/one-number-per-cell).
